# Get to the Chopper!

PS1-style mobile escape action game (Godot 4.7). This repo is the **proof of concept**: it exists to find out whether the 20–30 seconds between route choices is fun.

- Design baseline: [`docs/design/handover.md`](docs/design/handover.md)
- What's locked, proposed or open: [`docs/design/decision-log.md`](docs/design/decision-log.md)

## Play it on your iPhone

Every push to `main` runs the tests, exports a web build and publishes it to GitHub Pages (`.github/workflows/web-deploy.yml`).

1. Open `https://<your-username>.github.io/<repo-name>/` in **Safari** on the iPhone.
2. For fullscreen: Share → **Add to Home Screen**, then launch it from the icon.
3. After a new push, wait for the Action to go green (~2–3 min), then reload.

Controls: swipe left/right to change lane, up to jump, down to slide; hold FIRE (bottom right) to shoot. Run into cover to take it; swipe left/right to break cover. At a fork, the far-left or far-right lane takes that side exit (corridor, stairs or ladder); the middle 3 lanes carry straight on. Tap to start and to retry, and to skip the boss's KO replay. On desktop: arrow keys, Enter.

## Run it on your computer

1. Install [Godot 4.7.x](https://godotengine.org/download) (standard build, not .NET).
2. Godot → Import → pick `project.godot` → Run (F5).
3. Tweak feel in `game/config/default_tuning.tres` (Inspector): lane count, speeds, swipe threshold, junction slowdown, targeting mode.

## Layout

```
game/
  autoload/     GameState (alert, run lifecycle), RunLog (route travelled, discovery)
  config/       Tuning resource: every number worth playtesting
  input/        SwipeInput: swipes + taps (+ keyboard on desktop)
  player/       Player scene + auto-run movement
  enemies/      one folder per enemy (rifle_trooper/, rusher_dog/, security_trooper/, pursuit_squad/, ...)
  route/        RouteGraph (data) + RouteRunner (walks it, commits choices)
  systems/      alert/, targeting/, extraction/
  ui/           hud/ (in-run HUD), menu/ (main menu, settings, pause, end screens), kit/ (the look and pixel font), route_map/ (the end screen's route map)
  audio/        sound synthesis (synth.gd), every sound (sound_bank.gd), playback and music (audio_director.gd)
  rendering/    shared PS1 materials, mood lighting (ambience.gd)
  levels/       one folder per mission: scene + route.json
assets/         shaders/psx, textures, models, audio, fonts
docs/           design/ (handover, decision log), dev/
tests/          unit tests + bot playthroughs (run in CI)
```

Conventions: snake_case files, PascalCase `class_name`s, one feature per folder (scene + script together). Numbers go in `Tuning`, not in code. Routes are data, never hard-coded.

## Tests

```
GODOT=/path/to/godot tests/run_all.sh
```
- Unit tests: route validity, alert gating, lane→exit rule, route geometry rules, swipe direction, targeting priority, trooper shot rules (dodge, cover, alert), alert tiers (sparse Alert 1, dogs only from Alert 2, one alarm runner), alarm runner rules, pursuit squad rules (cover, catching), searchlight rules, sniper rules (the hit, following then locking, the window by alert, quiet at SNEAKING, can't be shot, where he may go), boss rules (2 free lanes, any pair, a new pattern for a new seed, a round that reaches you hits including a sidestep across one, never a round into a free lane, the rounds leave the tip of the gun and fan out across the lanes, the flash lights up, the cases land on the deck, his feet planted flat as he turns, the spin-up by alert, dodging versus standing in a swept lane, down after 30 hits; the KO replay: his death the same at the same moment however often it is shown, like a ragdoll with no joint flipping round from one moment to the next, his waist bending but never wringing round and him settling still once down, twice round in the air, face up a couple of metres back in one pool of blood with none of him (toes, fingertips, skull) through the deck, the gun dropped in front of where he stood clear of the lane you run past in, three slowed shots from three angles with him in view on a tall phone, under the rotor, about 9 s and skippable), chopper stages, cover rules (wall width, box material, no trooper hidden behind a wall, no forced swipe-and-jump, no wall on the inside of a corner), no overlapping objects, stairs only one level (tunnels - main - rooftops).
- Bot playthroughs: a scripted player runs twenty-five routes and must die, extract straight on along the whole ground route, take the MAIN FLOOR's stairs down through the underground and the STORM DRAIN's ladder out, find the stairs down locked at Alert 2, shoot the alarm box to reopen them, drop from the WAREHOUSE into the PUMP STATION, go up to the ROOFTOPS and back down into the SECURITY WING, ladder down from the ROOF EDGE, be captured for missing the ladder, switch back to the middle at the last moment and still go straight on, take a shot in cover without being hurt, camp in cover until the chopper leaves, run into a barrier and still get out (a stumble, not death), get bitten by the guard dog and still get out, dodge the dog out of its lane, let the alarm runner reach his alarm (Alert 2), trip both main-route wires (Alert 3, outrunning the squad), hide in cover at Alert 3 and be caught by the squad, run the roofs without dodging the searchlights (spotted up to Alert 3), hold still for a roof sniper and take the hit (the other bots dodge every sniper, each in view on a tall phone screen when he locks on), and shoot both alarm boxes back to Alert 1; every route that reaches the helipad fights the boss (stepping into a free lane each spin-up), one takes a sweep to prove it hits, one never shoots him and watches the chopper leave, one switches to TAP ONLY aiming and taps him to shoot him, and one taps to skip his KO replay; every KO replay is checked to hand back normal time, the camera and the chopper's countdown (not moved), with him in view on a tall phone throughout. If you edit `route.json` and make it unwinnable, CI fails.
